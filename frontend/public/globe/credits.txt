TrendProof globe imagery and boundaries

Daytime Earth, nighttime Earth, and terrain relief are the 4K Blue Marble, 4K night, and 2K topography assets distributed with Vasturiano’s three-globe examples. NASA Earth Observatory is credited for the Blue Marble imagery.
https://github.com/vasturiano/three-globe/tree/master/example/img
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/

Cloud layer: Tom Patterson’s Natural Earth III fair-weather clouds, in the transparent PNG prepared by Bjørn Sandvik for his WebGL Earth experiment. The globe.gl cloud example distributes that same PNG. Natural Earth III imagery is in the public domain.
https://www.shadedrelief.com/natural3/pages/clouds.html
https://www.shadedrelief.com/natural3/pages/use.html
https://github.com/turban/webgl-earth
https://github.com/vasturiano/globe.gl/tree/master/example/clouds

Country shapes: Made with Natural Earth. The 1:110 million-scale Admin 0 polygons come from the react-globe.gl example dataset, are generalized for visualization, and follow Natural Earth’s de facto boundaries. Their names and country codes help match city searches to shapes. Natural Earth vector data is in the public domain.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vasturiano/react-globe.gl/master/example/datasets/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-admin-0-countries/
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The static Earth image is NASA Earth Observatory’s Blue Marble Eastern Hemisphere. Image by Reto Stöckli, with enhancements by Robert Simmon at NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The globe combines archival imagery with simulated lighting and cloud motion.
